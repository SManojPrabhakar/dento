Detailed Guide: Types of Human Teeth

1. Incisors (8 total)

Front sharp, chisel-shaped teeth used for cutting and biting food into smaller manageable pieces.

2. Canines (4 total)

Pointy, cusp-shaped teeth situated next to incisors designed for tearing and gripping tough foods.

3. Premolars (8 total)

Transition teeth with flat surfaces positioned between canines and molars for crushing and grinding.

4. Molars (12 total including Wisdom teeth)

Large, broad back teeth specialized for thoroughly grinding and chewing food before swallowing.
